# C2: Closing a Request with a result, and the Impact chart

## Covered

- **Data made through the app (Northwind Walk 228).** Six Requests, all filed by `pm` in the New Request composer, then taken through Pick up → Mark Done → "Is it live?" → Yes, record release → Record release dialog → Close Request / Close without a result… on the Request page:
  - LAN-40 "[C2] Checkout: keep the card form filled after a payment error": Conversion rate 62% → 80%, 14 days, released 10 Aug 2026, closed at **84% (beat the target)**, with a long "What we learned" line.
  - LAN-41 "[C2] Checkout: show the delivery date before payment": Conversion rate 40% → 55%, 14 days, released 24 Aug, closed at **47% (missed)**, then **corrected to 46%** through Add correction (offline first, then online).
  - LAN-42 "[C2] Support: refund requests answered on the same day": a new metric I added from the picker with a **long name**, "[C2] Median minutes until a customer's refund request is resolved by the support team" (Minutes), 240 → 90, 7 days, released 14 Sep, closed at 75 minutes. That metric is now in the workspace's Metrics library.
  - LAN-45 "[C2] Keyboard: reach every checkout control without a mouse": **Pass/fail check** (Keyboard navigation), 7 days, released 21 Sep, closed **Passed**.
  - LAN-43 "[C2] Checkout: remember the chosen shipping speed": Conversion rate 55% → 60%, released 5 Oct, **closed without a result** (Rolled back, with details).
  - LAN-44 "[C2] Checkout: shorter address form on phones": Conversion rate 48% → 58%, 14 days, released 1 Oct, closed at 52% (missed). It gives Q4 2026 its own chart.
  - Daniel (`dev`) picked up, marked Done and recorded the release for LAN-40/41/42/45. Priya (`pm`) did all of that for LAN-43/44. Priya closed all six (only the Requester can close).
- **Dates I moved (SQL against `lane_test_dev_228`, only these six rows):** `requests.created_at` for LAN-40, 41, 42, 43, 44, 45, from their real creation time (26 Oct 2026, 09:28 to 09:40 UTC) back to `2026-08-01 09:00:00 UTC`. Nothing else was changed in the database. The release form will not accept a release date earlier than the creation date, so this let me type past release dates (10 Aug, 24 Aug, 14 Sep, 21 Sep, 1 Oct, 5 Oct) in the app's own Record release dialog. The due dates and closures then came out naturally, as each measurement window had already ended. Side effect: each timeline reads "Requested … on 1 Aug 2026" and "Overdue N days".
- **Closing flow walked** at 1440 light (LAN-40, 44), 390 dark (LAN-41), 1440 dark (LAN-42), 768 light (LAN-45), 1024 light (LAN-43); release dialogs at the same sizes. Checked: keyboard opening, Cancel, Escape, focus after open/cancel/save, axe (0 violations on every dialog and form), overflow (0), the time from save until the Closed step shows, the correction form while offline (good: "Check your connection and try again.", input kept), and the closed-step view.
- **`/impact` with results**, as `pm` and `dev`: Outcome report for Q3 2026 (two charts plus a pass/fail line), Q4 2026 (one chart, Closed without a result grouped by reason), Year 2026 (Conversion rate chart with 3 Requests), August 2026 (month: no totals by design), July 2026 (empty: "Nothing was Released in July 2026."). Also My work with closed results (dev), and teammate views (dev → Mei Lin, pm → Daniel at 390 dark). Checked 1440 light, 1440 dark, 1024 light with reduced motion (the bars' grow animation is off: good), 768 dark, 390 dark. Hover over bars, Tab through the report, axe (0 violations everywhere), overflow (0 everywhere), and the chart's screen-reader text. The chart is a single image whose screen-reader name lists every value, e.g. "Conversion rate, unit %, window 14 days after release. Request #40 …: before release 62 %, actual 84 %, predicted 80 %."
- **Dark mode chart:** readable. Bars are bright green / blue / pink on the near-black card. The 12px legend is light grey on dark, about 6:1. So the seeded "Dark mode: chart labels are hard to read" is not a problem on screen. The real problem is that the chart has no labels at all (C2-2).
- Already logged by C, also seen here and not repeated: account name vs profile name (C8: the Journey says "Requester: Review PM" and "recorded by Review Developer" next to "Requested by Priya Raman"); one-line values centred against two-line neighbours in Outcome report rows (C30; a long metric name makes it much worse, wrapping to 5 lines at 1440, [impact-q3-scroll3-1440-light](shots/C2/impact-q3-scroll3-1440-light.webp)); the tab not kept in the address (C16).
