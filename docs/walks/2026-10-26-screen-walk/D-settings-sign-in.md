# D: Settings, sign-in/sign-up, onboarding and public pages

## Covered

- **Settings as `pm` (Admin), Northwind:** Profile (account details, Manage account, Manage connected accounts, Role auto-save incl. offline), Preferences (theme System/Light/Dark), Inbox delivery (Email/Off, incl. offline), Security & access (Manage security), Workspace details (Copy ID, Manage workspace), Members (inline invite: empty/invalid/long email, no invitation sent; role menu opened and closed with Escape; "Manage members in Clerk" opened and closed), Metrics list, Add metric dialog (empty, bad link, created `[D] Walk metric alpha` and `[D] Walk metric beta …long name…`), metric detail (empty name, Archive and Restore, Merge alpha into beta: confirmed, both mine), Billing, Coming soon, the sidebar, section switching with Back/Forward, "/" search (results, no results, a Coming soon result), ⌘K "billing", Back to Requests.
- **Settings as `dev` (Member):** every section, `/settings/members`, `/settings/metrics`, a metric detail, and `?section=billing|slack` typed by hand.
- **Empty workspace (`empty`):** Members, Metrics, Billing.
- **Sizes and themes:** 1440 light (all), 390 dark (all sections, metrics, metric detail, Add metric, Clerk account modal, search), 1024 dark and 768 light (overflow checks plus spot screenshots). No horizontal overflow anywhere in Settings. axe found nothing in Settings.
- **Signed out:** `/login`, `/signup`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password` (no token, so it shows the forgot form), `/privacy`, `/terms` at 1440 light and dark and 390 dark. Error states: empty submit, bad format, unknown email on login, signup and forgot password. Sign-up was not completed.
